# Downloaded city scenery

The city uses real downloaded assets, served locally so visitors do not depend on a marketplace API or account. All included assets permit redistribution under CC0. The original sixteen Project 0 lots keep their own exterior models and numbers.

- **Kay Lousberg / KayKit — City Builder Bits 1.0.** [itch.io listing](https://kaylousberg.itch.io/city-builder-bits), [official source repository](https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-City-Builder-Bits-1.0). Six city building choices, benches, streetlights, bushes, rubbish bins, hydrants and parked cars. Official glTF files and their palette texture were packed into self-contained GLBs with Blender; no paid/source pack was used. [Included license](../public/assets/city-upgrade/licenses/kaykit.txt).
- **Kenney — Nature Kit 2.1.** [Creator page](https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit). Oak and round pine models replace the simple placeholder trees. GLBs are unchanged from the official free archive. [Included license](../public/assets/city-upgrade/licenses/kenney-nature.txt).
- **Poly Haven — photographed surfaces and HDR skies.** [Asphalt 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/asphalt_02), [Concrete Pavers](https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_pavers), and [Grass Ground](https://polyhaven.com/a/grass_ground) provide original 1K diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness maps. [Qwantani Sunset Pure Sky](https://polyhaven.com/a/qwantani_sunset_puresky), photographed by Greg Zaal and processed by Jarod Guest, supplies the current warm sky and reflections. The earlier [Kloppenheim 06 Pure Sky](https://polyhaven.com/a/kloppenheim_06_puresky) remains in the source archive. All are [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license); visitors need no marketplace account.

[Asset manifest](../public/assets/city-upgrade/manifest.json) records each included file's source, license, byte size and SHA-256 digest. The 26 asset files total 10.18 MB before HTTP compression; the sunset and photographed ground/paving update added 5.58 MB. Runtime loads the current HDR, shared surfaces and nearby buildings as needed.

## Runtime

Road and sidewalk surfaces are merged into a draw call per surface, with consistent world-scale UVs. Ground, vacant lots and planted plaza islands share the grass material. Each downloaded street prop/tree primitive is instanced across the city, sharing geometry and materials. A gentle material tint makes the pastel tree palette less blue without editing its downloaded textures. The active 1K HDR is prefiltered once for reflections. Shadows update when scenery changes or the player travels rather than every frame. Buildings load near the player and use a fixed local whitelist. Download failures keep the procedural building/vegetation/sky fallback; the catalog, flight controls and projects remain usable.

The sunset HDR's brightest sun pixel points along `[0.8040, 0.10717, 0.58489]` before scene rotation (36.04° heading, 6.15° elevation). Its background and environment rotate together using `CITY_LOOK.environment.rotation`; the directional sunlight follows the same heading. This keeps warm facade lighting, reflections and the visible sky coherent. Surface albedo uses sRGB; normal and roughness maps remain linear.

New approved community entries can select `lotStyle` from `studio`, `arcade`, `gallery`, `workshop`, `tower`, or `garden`. This selects a reviewed local building asset and never executes a submitted script. An original project exterior always takes precedence over this choice.
